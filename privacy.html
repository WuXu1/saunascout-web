Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Short version: this website collects no personal data and uses no cookies. The app keeps only what it needs to work, never sells it, and lets you export or delete it.

The website (saunascout.app)

This is a single information page about the SaunaScout app. It has no accounts, no forms, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking scripts. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host, GitHub processes standard server request logs, including visitors' IP addresses, for security and to serve the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages data collection. We don't have access to those logs and don't combine them with anything else.

If you email us, we receive your message and email address and use them only to reply.

Cookies

This website uses no cookies of any kind, so there is no cookie banner and nothing to consent to. If we ever add anything that needs cookies (for example website analytics), we'll update this page and ask for consent first where the law requires it.

The SaunaScout app

This section covers the SaunaScout mobile app. We are the data controller for the information described here. Contact details are at the bottom of the page.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, first name, username, age group and home town. You can sign up with email, Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google; if you use Apple or Google we receive only what they share with us (typically your name and email, or Apple's private relay address).
  • Profile: an optional profile photo and banner.
  • Your activity: the saunas you save or log, visit dates, ratings, temperatures, notes, any photos or videos you attach, and the peaks you bag.
  • Community content: posts, comments, likes, follows, friend connections and direct messages.
  • Location: your device location, only if you allow it, to show you on the map and nearby saunas. Location is off by default. We don't keep a history of where you have been.
  • Push notification token: if you allow notifications, so we can send them to your device.
  • Suggestions and reports: anything you send us about a sauna, a listing or another user.
  • Business listings: if you claim a sauna as its owner or operator, the business details, contact details, images and opening hours you provide, which are shown publicly on that listing.
  • Technical data: our backend provider records your IP address and request times to keep the service secure and working.

Why we use it

To run your account, show your logbook and progress, power the Community and notifications, keep the service secure, and fix problems. We don't use your data for advertising and we don't sell it.

What other people can see

Your logbook and your followers and following lists are private until you choose to share them in Settings → Profile visibility. Anything you post publicly in the Community is visible to other users. Direct messages are visible only to the people in the conversation, and to us if a message is reported.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase hosts our database, authentication and file storage on our behalf.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, maps and push notifications, and Google provides Sign in with Google, when you use them.
  • Expo delivers app updates.

These providers process data on our instructions. We don't share your data with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. If you delete your account, we remove your profile and content. Some copies may remain in backups for a short time before they are overwritten.

Your choices

  • Edit or remove your profile details at any time in the app.
  • Settings → Export my data gives you a copy of your information.
  • Settings → Delete account permanently removes your account and content.
  • You can turn off location and notifications at any time in your phone's settings.

Children

SaunaScout is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have rights over any personal data we hold about you, including access, correction, and deletion. The website itself holds none. For anything else, contact us.
