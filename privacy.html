Who we are

SaunaScout ("we", "us") operates the SaunaScout mobile app and this website at saunascout.co.uk.

Information we collect

  • Account details — email address and display name when you create an account.
  • Location — your device location, only while using the map, to show nearby saunas. Used on-device and not stored unless you choose to save a visit.
  • Content you submit — sauna suggestions, visits you log, and any community posts or messages.
  • Usage & diagnostics — basic app analytics and crash logs to keep the app working.

How we use it

To run your account, show relevant saunas, sync your progress, review community submissions, and improve the app. We do not sell your personal data.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with our backend provider (Supabase). Sub-processors and data-retention periods will be listed here before launch.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by emailing us. You can delete your account from within the app.
